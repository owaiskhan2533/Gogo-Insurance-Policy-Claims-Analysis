# Gogo Insurance Pvt. Ltd. - Policy & Claims Analytics Dashboard 
An interactive Power BI dashboard for Gogo Insurance Pvt. Ltd. analyzing premium distribution, policy active/inactive status, claim settlement analysis, and customer demographics.


##  Project Overview
This **Power BI Analytics Dashboard** provides key business insights for **Gogo Insurance Pvt. Ltd.** by tracking policy sales, claim distributions, customer demographics, and overall financial metrics. It helps management monitor revenue generation via premiums, evaluate policy retention, and optimize claim settlement processes.

---

##  Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* Total Premium Amount: 5.98M
* Total Coverage Amount: 600.55M
* Total Claim Amount: 16.91M
* Gender Ratio: Female (5,001) vs Male (5,003)

---

## Tools & Technologies Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** DAX Calculations, Data Modeling, Custom Dark/Neon Theme Dashboard.
* **Filters/Slicers:** Interactive slicing by `PolicyNumber`, `ClaimNumber`, and `CustomerID`.

---

##  Dashboard Visualizations & Key Features

1. **Premium Amount by Policy Type:**
   * Breakdowns across Travel (2.5M), Health (1.2M), Auto (1.0M), Life (0.7M), and Home (0.6M) policies.
2. **Active vs. Inactive Policies:**
   * Tracks policy retention with **58.13% Active (5.82K)** vs **41.87% Inactive (4.19K)** policies.
3. **Number of Claims by Claim Status:**
   * Breakdown of claims by **Rejected (4.4K)**, **Settled (3.4K)**, and **Pending (2.3K)** statuses.
4. **Claim Amount by Age Group:**
   * Analysis of claim distribution across **Adults (8.8M)**, **Elders (6.4M)**, and **Young Adults (1.7M)**.
5. **Policy Type vs. Claim Status Matrix:**
   * Granular table analyzing Pending, Rejected, and Settled claim amounts across all policy categories.

##  Key Business Insights
* **Top Revenue Generator:** **Travel Insurance** generates the highest premium amount (2.5M), followed by Health Insurance (1.2M).
* **Claim Rejections:** Rejection rates are significantly high (4.4K claims rejected vs 3.4K settled), signaling a need to review policy verification criteria.
* **Demographic Risk:** Adults account for the largest proportion of claim amounts (8.8M), requiring tailored risk management strategies for middle-aged policyholders.
